01The record

Sixty years from the degree to the citation list.

Dr. Toth took his medical degree at Semmelweis University in Budapest in 1965. He was certified by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology in 1983.

His work sits at the intersection of two fields that are usually kept apart: obstetrics and gynecology on one side, infectious disease on the other. The published record is about what happens when they meet.
